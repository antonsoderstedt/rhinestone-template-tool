# ADR 0040: Papercraft models and repairing an imported model (phase 4)

**Status:** accepted · **Date:** 2026-11-25 · Builds on ADR 0037 to 0039.

## Why

Ten built-in shapes (mostly solids from geometry class) are a poor first impression of a tool for paper models, and an imported file with a
hole in it just gave a warning and no way out.

## Decision

- **Twenty built-in models in four groups** (Shapes, Hearts stars and gems, Things, Animals), each with a small picture drawn from the model
  itself (`projectSolid`, so there are no image files to keep). New: heart box, star box, vase, bottle, lantern, house, egg, rocket, fish and swan.
  All are generated by us from three small generators: `lathe` (solids of revolution), `extrude` (an outline pushed up) and the new `sweep`
  (a body swept along a curve in the front view, with fins: fish, swan). There are no third-party models.
- **Built-ins must be good first models.** A test builds every one at its default size and requires: a clean mesh (no warnings), no edge
  shorter than 8 mm (the studio warns under that: too small to fold and glue), at most 100 faces, no pattern problem except a few edges without a
  tab (at most 40 % of the glued edges). The shapes were simplified until they met this (fewer sides, no tiny bevels, thicker necks).
- **Check and fix for imported files.** The studio shows what is wrong with an imported model (open edges, a twisted surface, separate pieces,
  edges shared by more than two faces) and offers two fixes: **close the holes** (`fillHoles`: every loop of open edges becomes one polygon if it is
  flat, otherwise triangles around its middle; loops over 400 corners are left open as they are more likely an open side of the model than a
  hole) and **turn the model inside out** (`flipFaces`, for an open model where the program cannot tell which side is out). A fix replaces the
  imported mesh, resets the hand edits and says what it did. Closed models are already turned outward automatically.
- **A size warning for every model:** a shortest edge under 8 mm says the model is too small to fold and glue well.
- **Tab placement works from the tightest edge first** (an edge with little room gets it before the roomy ones fill it up) and has a last-resort
  triangular tab. Edges that still have no room are reported (`tab-missing`): glue them edge to edge. On curved models a few edges, in the same part, had
  no room on either side; ADR 0042 fixed most of them.

## Not in this phase

Simplifying a heavy model to low poly, removing tiny faces, a fix for non-orientable or self-intersecting surfaces, a model made from the tool's 2D
shapes (extrude or lathe an artwork), and low-poly animals with legs (those are several bodies and need joining).

## Consequences

- The fish and the swan are simple (a four-sided fish, a five-sided swan) so their edges are big enough to glue; they read as low-poly figures, not
  as detailed animals. The owner's own models are what the studio is for.
- The faceted bodies are made of triangles where the corners of a ring are not flat; neighbours that turn out flat are merged again.
