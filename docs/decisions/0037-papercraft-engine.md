# ADR 0037: Papercraft engine (3D model to printable and cuttable pattern)

**Status:** accepted · **Date:** 2026-11-25

## Why

The owner wants a third studio, Papercraft, next to Rhinestone and HTV: turn a low-poly 3D model into a paper pattern that is printed
or cut and scored with a Cricut Maker 3. The plan and the decisions made with the owner are in `docs/PAPERCRAFT_PLAN.md`. This ADR
records the engine decisions; the algorithm is in `docs/PAPERCRAFT_ENGINE_SPEC.md`.

## Decision

- A separate engine, `src/lib/papercraft-engine/`, with the same rules as the Rhinestone engine: pure functions, millimetres, no DOM,
  no network, no `Math.random()`/`Date.now()`, same input gives the same output. It does not import from `/app/`, and the Rhinestone
  engine does not import from it.
- **Unfolding by greedy island merging.** Every face starts as its own part; edges are visited from the most worth keeping joined (long,
  nearly flat) and two parts are joined across an edge only if the result has no overlap and fits the page. This gives a spanning forest
  with no overlap by construction. A spanning tree followed by splitting (the usual approach) was rejected: it needs a repair step, and
  the greedy merge already respects a page-size limit.
- **Flat faces stay flat.** Connected coplanar triangles are merged into one polygon, so a cube from an STL file is 6 squares, not 12
  triangles with diagonal folds.
- **Tabs are chosen, not drawn blindly.** A tab is accepted only if it does not overlap any face or tab of its part; width is reduced,
  then side angles sharpened, then the other side of the pair tried. An edge with no room is reported, not hidden.
- **Exact overlap tests with a touching tolerance** (0.1 µm): faces that only touch along an edge or at a corner are not overlapping.
- **Output: PDF (vector, no embedded fonts, no dates; the cover page's text uses the built-in Helvetica, ADR 0039) and SVG in mm.** Numbers are drawn with a built-in stroke font (lines), so
  Cricut Design Space and printers need no font. The SVG has `cut`, `score` and `numbers` groups in three colours for Cricut, and dashed
  mountain/valley groups for print. No raster, no `<use>`, no nested `<svg>`, no opacity.
- **Models**: OBJ and STL are read in the engine; GLB is read in the app with three.js and handed over as vertices and faces. Built-in
  shapes are generated by us. The owner's PDF templates are third-party licensed and are never used as built-in examples or committed.
- Mountain and valley follow one setting, `printedSide` (outside by default): a convex corner seen from the printed side is a mountain
  fold. To be confirmed on a physical test cube.

## Consequences

- The number of parts for a given model is whatever the greedy merge gives; it will not match Pepakura's hand-tuned nets for complex
  models. The studio lets the owner force an edge cut or joined (`forceCutEdges`, `forceJoinEdges`).
- Cricut scoring, the usable mat area and tab and fold tolerances are unverified until a test sheet has been cut.
- Page packing uses bounding boxes of the convex hull (MaxRects). Parts nest less tightly than with true polygon nesting.
- Parts bigger than a page are split by the unfolding itself; a single face bigger than the page is reported and not split.
